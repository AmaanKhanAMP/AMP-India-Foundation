# Volunteer with AMP India Foundation

Join AMP India Foundation as a volunteer and contribute your talent to social development while gaining meaningful experience.

## How to volunteer

1. Visit the Volunteer page on the website: /volunteer
2. Register through the secure volunteer registration portal.
3. Share your skills and preferred areas of contribution.
4. The team typically responds within a few working days.

**Registration URL:** https://tinyurl.com/AIFVolunteerRegn

## Areas of volunteering

- Education support and mentoring
- Employment / job fair assistance
- Healthcare and medical camp support
- Community outreach and events
- Career guidance and soft-skill coaching
- Local field support based on your expertise

## Benefits

- Meaningful social impact experience
- Opportunity to mentor students and job seekers
- Collaboration with professionals and student mentors
- Contribution to education, employment, healthcare, and empowerment programs
