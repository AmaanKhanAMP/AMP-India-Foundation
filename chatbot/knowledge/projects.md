# Projects & Programs — AMP India Foundation

AMP India Foundation runs education, employment, medical, empowerment, mentorship, and training initiatives for underprivileged youth and communities.

## Flagship Programs

- **ACE - Academy for Competitive Exams** — Premium coaching and mentorship for competitive exam aspirants.
- **AMP Employment Assistance Cell** — Connecting skilled youth with employers through training and job fairs.
- **National Talent Search (NTS)** — Identifying and nurturing talented students across India.
- **AMP Higher Education Scholarship** — Scholarships enabling meritorious students to pursue higher education.

## Initiatives

### Education & Vocational Skills
Vocational skill programs for BPL youth including trades such as mobile repair, AC/fridge servicing, water filter systems, and vehicle-related skills, with NSDC and CSR partnerships.

### Medical Projects
Diagnostic camps, free medicine clinics, and emergency tertiary-care funding for underserved communities.

### Employment Support
Job fairs, resume clinics, mock interviews, and employer partnerships that help skilled youth find work.

### Mentorship & Centres of Excellence
Professional mentoring, coaching, and career guidance for meritorious but underprivileged students.
