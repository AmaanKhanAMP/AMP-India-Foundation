# What is AMP India Foundation (AIF)?

AMP India Foundation (also called AIF or AMP) is a registered non-profit organisation that delivers structured social services for underprivileged communities across India. It is managed through professional volunteer resources.

Aliases people use: AMP, AIF, AMP India, AMP India Foundation, Amp Foundation.

## Mission

AMP India Foundation is dedicated to promoting inclusive social progression by bridging structural development gaps for the under-served and needy all over India, entirely managed through professional volunteer resources.

## Vision

Trusted professional philanthropy that uplifts communities through education, employment, skill development, medical relief, and mentorship—without bias of caste, community, creed, or religion.

## Core Values

- Trusted Professional Philanthropy — managed voluntarily by verified industry professionals
- Good Will Volunteers — nationwide volunteer network for critical care and field support
- Centres of Excellence — incubation hubs for under-served students with guidance and scholarships

## Objectives

- Education and vocational skills training for a sustainable future
- Scholarships and financial support for higher education and orphans' basic education
- Self-employment assistance and livelihood support
- Critical illness and medical services support
- Mentoring, coaching and career guidance through Centres of Excellence
- Crowdfunding platforms connecting donors and help-seekers
- Community upliftment without religious or caste bias

## Focus Areas

1. Economic Empowerment — financial literacy, micro-grants, self-help groups
2. Employment Support — job seekers, career opportunities, job fairs
3. Skill Development — vocational training for modern industry needs
4. Medical Relief — emergency medical funds, health camps, critical care
5. Student Mentorship — career counseling and academic pathways

## Is AMP India Foundation trustworthy / genuine?

Yes. AMP India Foundation is a registered non-profit organisation focused on transparent, professional philanthropy. Programs are designed for underprivileged communities and managed through verified professional volunteers. For official verification, donors and beneficiaries may contact the foundation through published phone, email, and the website Support Us / Contact pages. Direct bank donations use published Kotak Mahindra account details for AMP India Foundation.

## What does AMP India Foundation do?

AMP India Foundation works in education, employment assistance, skill development, medical relief, scholarships, mentorship, volunteering, and community events such as job fairs and medical camps.
