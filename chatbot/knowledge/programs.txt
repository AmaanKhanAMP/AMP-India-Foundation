# AMP India Foundation Programs and Services

AMP India Foundation (AIF) provides the following services and social initiatives:

## Education
- Higher education scholarships for meritorious underprivileged students
- Orphans' basic education support
- Centres of Excellence for mentoring and academic guidance
- Scholarship awareness workshops

## Employment
- AMP Employment Assistance Cell
- Job fairs and placement drives
- Resume clinics and mock interviews
- Career counselling by industry professionals
- Connections between skilled youth and employers

## Skill Development
- Vocational training for BPL youth (mobile repair, AC/fridge servicing, and related trades)
- Employability Training Programme (ETP)
- NSDC and CSR partnership-oriented skill programs

## Medical / Social Initiatives
- Diagnostic camps and free medicine clinics
- Emergency tertiary-care funding
- Critical illness support
- Community outreach and health screening camps

## Mentorship
- ACE – Academy for Competitive Exams
- National Talent Search (NTS)
- Professional mentoring and career guidance

## Other Support
- Volunteer network and community service opportunities
- Crowdfunding for education, medical relief, and livelihoods
- Donation / Support Us channel for philanthropic contributions
