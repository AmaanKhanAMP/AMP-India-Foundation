# Scholarship Programs — AMP India Foundation

AMP India Foundation offers scholarships and financial support so meritorious, underprivileged students can pursue education and build sustainable livelihoods.

## Programs

### AMP Higher Education Scholarship
Scholarships enabling meritorious students to pursue higher education.

### Orphans' Basic Education Support
Financial assistance supporting basic education for orphaned children.

### Self-Employment Assistance
Support that helps beneficiaries start or grow livelihood opportunities.

### Critical Illness & Medical Support
Financial help for critical illness and essential medical services.

## How to Apply

Deserving students can apply through AMP's designated online crowdfunding and welfare application portals. Submissions are transparently verified based strictly on merit and economic need criteria.
