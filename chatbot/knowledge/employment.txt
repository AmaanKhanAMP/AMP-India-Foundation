# Employment Assistance and Job Support — AMP India Foundation (AIF)

AMP India Foundation helps youth find jobs and prepare for careers. This is employment support, not volunteering.

## How AMP helps with jobs

- Job fairs and mega placement drives
- On-spot interviews and hiring opportunities with corporate employers
- Resume clinics and CV preparation support
- Mock interviews and interview preparation
- Career guidance and counselling by industry professionals
- AMP Employment Assistance Cell connecting skilled youth with employers
- Employability Training Programme (ETP) for pre-employment readiness

Current job fairs and training dates are listed on the website Events page and in the CMS. Do not use dated placeholder events from older knowledge files.

## How to participate / what to do for a job

1. Follow AMP India Foundation events and Employment Assistance Cell announcements
2. Prepare an updated resume/CV
3. Attend job fairs, placement drives, and training programmes
4. Use resume clinics and mock interview support at employment events
5. For questions, call +91 8291101312 or email contact@ampindiafoundation.org

## Common questions

Q: What jobs do you provide?
A: AMP does not run a permanent job portal listing daily openings. It provides employment assistance through job fairs, placement drives, employer connections, resume support, and interview preparation.

Q: Mujhe naukri ke liye kaise madad milegi?
A: Aap AMP ke job fair, placement drive, resume clinic aur Employment Assistance Cell ke through madad le sakte hain. Latest dates Events page / CMS par dekhein.

Q: Job ke liye kya karna padega?
A: Updated resume banayein, AMP employment events me participate karein, mock interview / resume clinic use karein, aur foundation se contact karke latest drive details lein.
